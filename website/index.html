Dictation took two people

For most of a century, one person spoke into a machine and another put on headphones and typed it up, ums and all, then tidied it by hand.

Parla does both jobs on your Mac, the moment you let go of the key.

The typist now lives on your Mac, and it never tires of “um”.

Your voice stays here

Parla turns speech into text on your Mac. No account, no server of ours, no sync. The only thing that can leave is the text, and only if you turn on AI cleanup, to the provider you pick, on your own key.

The AI is yours too. You can bring a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq, Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible service, pick any model it offers, and switch whenever you like. The request goes straight from your Mac to them, with no middleman and no markup.

Or run the model on your Mac with Ollama or LM Studio, and nothing leaves at all.

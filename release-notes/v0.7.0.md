Parla can now set up dictation differently for each app and website. Your mail site can get formal cleanup, a chat app can get a conversational tone, and your code editor can skip AI cleanup entirely. This release also adds saved rewrite and translation presets, voice templates, audio file import, a microphone test, on-device cleanup with Apple Intelligence, and Keychain storage for your API keys.

## Install

1. Download **Parla-0.7.0.dmg** below.
2. Open it and drag Parla into Applications.
3. Open Parla from Applications.

Runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13.3 or later.

## If you're upgrading from 0.6.0

Choose **Install Update v0.7.0** in the menu bar menu. Parla downloads the new version, swaps it in and reopens. Microphone and Accessibility permissions carry over. If you saved an AI cleanup API key in Parla, 0.7.0 moves it from settings.json into your Keychain.

## New

- **App & Site Profiles** in the Hub give an app (by bundle ID) or a website (by host) its own settings: AI cleanup on or off, cleanup style, spoken language, Whisper model, cleanup model and extra dictionary words. Anything you leave unset follows your global settings.
- **Output & Presets** sets what a dictation becomes: normal cleanup, the raw transcript with no rewriting, or a saved preset. A preset is your own rewrite instruction, like "make this more concise", or a translation into a language you choose. The optional **Run selected preset** shortcut (General → Shortcuts) applies it to text you've selected in any app, without recording.
- **Voice Templates…** in the menu bar menu lets you define a form with named fields, dictate each field in turn, review the finished document, then copy it or save it to History. Templates use the raw transcript, with no AI cleanup.
- **Import Audio** in the Hub transcribes a recording file instead of the microphone. It accepts mono 16-bit PCM WAV files, and MOV files whose audio is mono 16 kHz 16-bit PCM, up to 10 minutes and 20 MB. AAC, MP4 and other formats need converting first. You get the raw transcript to copy or save. **Keep a private copy and transcribe** keeps the file and its result in Parla for seven days so you can transcribe it again later.
- **Microphone Test** in the Hub records up to 10 seconds, shows the input level and plays it back, with no speech model needed. Nothing is transcribed or saved.
- **Apple Intelligence · on-device** is a new choice under AI Cleanup → Service. It needs no API key and sends no text to a server. It needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on, and you set **Declared source locale** to the language you dictate in. It only rewrites text it's confident is in that one language, so short or mixed-language dictations are inserted raw. Translation presets don't work with it.
- **API keys are stored in Keychain** instead of settings.json. The key field never shows your saved key: type a new one and choose **Save / Replace**, or choose **Remove**.
- **History** entries have **Copy Original**, **Copy Result**, **Delete** and **Export…**. **Review Retry Cleanup** runs cleanup again on the original with your current settings and shows the original, the old result and the new one before you apply it. **Export Filtered…** saves the entries matching your search as plain text or Markdown.
- **Saved text limit** in Data & Privacy sets how many dictations History keeps, from 1 to 1,000. The default is still 50.
- **Recovery audio** in Data & Privacy lists the audio Parla keeps from failed dictations. You can play a file, transcribe it again on this Mac, or delete it. You can also set how many days it's kept (1 to 30, default 7) or stop keeping it.
- The **Dictionary** puts your words in priority order: use the arrows, and when the list is longer than Parla can send, the top words win. **Literal replacements** always turn an exact word or phrase into the text you give. **Import…** and **Export…** move your words, snippets and replacements between Macs as one file, without API keys.
- **Discard pending** appears in the menu bar menu while a dictation is still being transcribed or cleaned up. Choose it to drop that result so nothing is typed.
- Model downloads can be paused and resumed, from the Hub or the menu bar menu, and they pick up where they stopped, even after you quit Parla.
- **Undo accidental cancellation** brings back a dictation you cancelled by mistake. It's off by default; turn it on in Data & Privacy. After you cancel a dictation of 10 seconds or less, the pill offers **Undo** for 3 seconds. Undo shows the raw transcript for 30 seconds so you can copy it. It works with Whisper models downloaded from Parla's list.
- **Diagnostics** in Data & Privacy shows the timings and outcomes of recent dictations, and you can export them as JSON for a bug report. They contain no text, audio, keys or app names, and they stay in memory until you quit.

## Fixed

- AI cleanup requests no longer follow HTTP redirects to another server. Before, a redirect could resend your text, and for Anthropic your API key, to a different site.
- If you started a new dictation while the previous one was still processing, and that earlier one came back empty or failed, its message could replace or hide the pill for the dictation you were recording. The pill now stays with your current dictation.

## Building from source

Still supported: `git checkout v0.7.0 && scripts/make-app.sh && open Parla.app`. A build from source can't install updates itself, since it isn't signed with the release's Developer ID, so its menu item opens the release page.

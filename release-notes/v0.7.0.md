Parla can now set up dictation differently for each app and website. Your mail site can get formal cleanup, a chat app can get a conversational tone, and your code editor can skip AI cleanup entirely. This release also adds meeting recording, undo for your last dictation, saved rewrite and translation presets, voice templates, audio file import, a microphone test, Apple Speech recognition, on-device cleanup with Apple Intelligence, backup cleanup services, and Keychain storage for your API keys.

## Install

1. Download **Parla-0.7.0.dmg** below.
2. Open it and drag Parla into Applications.
3. Open Parla from Applications.

Runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13.3 or later. A few features need a newer macOS; they say so below.

## If you're upgrading from 0.6.0

Choose **Install Update v0.7.0** in the menu bar menu. Parla downloads the new version, swaps it in and reopens. Microphone and Accessibility permissions carry over. If you saved an AI cleanup API key in Parla, 0.7.0 moves it from settings.json into your Keychain. If that can't finish, AI Cleanup tells you; choose **Refresh** there to try again.

## New

- **Meetings** in the Hub records your microphone, and if you like one app's audio too, then transcribes it on this Mac. Before each recording you confirm that everyone in the meeting knows it's being recorded. A recording can last up to an hour, and leaving the Meetings page stops it. Meetings stay on this Mac and aren't added to History. Unless you turn on **Keep this meeting until I delete it**, the audio is deleted one day after it's transcribed (seven days if it never is), and the transcript 30 days after the meeting started. Parla holds one saved meeting at a time: choose **Delete meeting…** to record another. Recording an app's audio needs macOS 14.2 or later and System Audio Recording access. Only its sound is recorded, not your screen.
- **Undo last dictation** and **Restore original text** in the menu bar menu work on your most recent dictation for 10 minutes. Undo takes out the text Parla typed. Restore original text swaps the cleaned-up text for exactly what you said. Both are on by default and work in ordinary text fields, never in password fields. You can use one of them once per dictation. If the text has changed since Parla typed it, or you've started a new dictation, Parla leaves the field alone and tells you.
- **Nearby text** in App & Site Profiles gives AI cleanup about 256 characters from just before your cursor, so it gets names and sentences right. Text after the cursor is never sent. Parla doesn't save nearby text, and for those dictations History keeps only your original words. With Apple Intelligence it stays on this Mac and is on by default; turn off **Use nearby text with Apple Intelligence** to stop it. For online cleanup it's off until you choose **Turn on…** next to an app profile, read exactly what will be sent and to which service, then choose **Send nearby text for this app**. Your provider may bill you for each request. Online, it works for one app at a time and not while backup cleanup is set up. Changing that profile, your cleanup settings or your dictionary pauses it until you turn it on again. It never runs in browsers, terminals, code editors, password managers, remote desktop apps or password fields, or while text is selected.
- **App & Site Profiles** in the Hub give an app (by bundle ID) or a website (by host) its own settings: AI cleanup on or off, cleanup style, spoken language, Whisper model, cleanup model and extra dictionary words. Anything you leave unset follows your global settings.
- **Output & Presets** sets what a dictation becomes: **Cleaned up**, **Original text** (no cleanup, snippets or replacements), or **Selected preset**. A preset is your own rewrite instruction, like "make this more concise", or a translation into a language you choose. The optional **Run selected preset** shortcut (General → Shortcuts) applies it to text you've highlighted in any app, without recording.
- **Mouse push to talk** (General → Shortcuts) lets you hold the middle mouse button, or a side button from 4 to 16, to dictate. It's off by default and needs Accessibility access. While it's set, that button's usual action, like Back, doesn't work.
- **Voice Templates…** in the menu bar menu lets you define a form with named fields, dictate each field in turn, review the finished document, then copy it or save it to History. Templates use your original words, without cleanup or snippets.
- **Import Audio** in the Hub transcribes a recording file instead of the microphone. It accepts mono 16-bit WAV files, and MOV files with uncompressed 16 kHz mono audio, up to 10 minutes and 20 MB. AAC, MP4 and other formats need converting first. You get the transcript to copy or save to History. **Save a copy and transcribe** keeps the file and its transcript on this Mac for seven days, even with History off, so you can transcribe it again later. Parla keeps up to 10 files this way.
- **Transcribe with timestamps** in Import Audio shows when each part was said, and **Export WebVTT…** or **Export SRT…** saves it as subtitles. It needs Whisper, a 16 kHz WAV file and a language chosen under General → Whisper model (not Auto-detect). Timestamps are approximate and are lost when you close the transcript.
- **Microphone Test** in the Hub records up to 10 seconds, shows the input level and plays it back. Nothing is transcribed or saved.
- **Apple Speech** is a new choice under General → Speech recognition → Engine. It needs macOS 26. Type the language you dictate in, like en-US, then choose **Set up language**; macOS may download it first. Your dictionary words are used for cleanup only, not for recognition. **Release language** frees a language you no longer use.
- **Show text while recording** (General → Live preview) shows draft text in the pill while you speak. It's off by default and needs a Whisper model. The draft is never typed, and the final text may differ. **Preview model** can use a smaller downloaded model for faster drafts.
- **Apple Intelligence (on this Mac)** is a new choice under AI Cleanup → Service. It needs no API key and sends no text to a server. It needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on, and you set **Language** to the language you dictate in. It only cleans up text it can confirm is in that language, so short or mixed-language dictations are typed as you said them. Translation presets don't work with it, and rewrite presets need **Keeps the same language** turned on.
- **Backup cleanup** on the AI Cleanup page tries up to two other services, in the order you set, when online cleanup fails. Each backup gets your text and may charge you. Add each one's provider, model and key, then choose **Review and turn on…** and confirm the services. It needs an online cleanup service with cleanup on and Dictation output set to Cleaned up, and it skips profiles that have their own cleanup model. Changing your cleanup service pauses backups until you review them again.
- **API keys are stored in Keychain** instead of settings.json. The key field never shows your saved key: type a new one and choose **Save**, or choose **Remove**.
- **History** entries have **Copy original**, **Copy result**, **Export…** and **Delete**. **Clean up again…** runs cleanup on the original with your current settings and shows the original, the previous result and the new one. Nothing is typed, and **Save result** replaces the entry in History. **Export (N)…** above the list saves the entries matching your search, original or result, as plain text or Markdown.
- **Clean up in sections…** in History cleans up a long dictation one section at a time and shows its progress. As with **Clean up again…**, nothing is typed and **Save result** replaces the entry. Each section is a separate request, so an online provider may bill you for each one. It isn't available while you have any snippets.
- **History limit** in Data & Privacy sets how many dictations History keeps, from 1 to 1,000. The default is still 50.
- **Failed dictations** in Data & Privacy lists the audio Parla keeps when a dictation fails. You can play a file, transcribe it again on this Mac, or delete it. You can also choose how many days it's kept (1 to 30, default 7), or turn off **Keep audio from failed dictations**.
- The **Dictionary** puts your words in priority order: use the arrows, and when the list is too long, the words near the top win. **Replacements** swap an exact word or phrase for the text you give. **Import…** and **Export…** move your words, snippets and replacements between Macs as one file, without API keys.
- **Discard current dictation** appears in the menu bar menu while a dictation is still being transcribed or cleaned up. Choose it to stop that result from being typed. Text that was already typed stays.
- Model downloads can be paused and resumed, from the Hub or the menu bar menu, and they pick up where they stopped, even after you quit Parla.
- **Allow undoing a canceled dictation** in Data & Privacy brings back a dictation you canceled by mistake. It's off by default. After you cancel a dictation of 10 seconds or less, the pill offers **Undo** for 3 seconds. Undo shows the transcript for 30 seconds so you can copy it. The audio stays in memory only. It works with Whisper models downloaded from Parla's list.
- **Diagnostics** in Data & Privacy shows the timings and outcomes of recent dictations. Choose **Review**, then **Export…** to save them as JSON for a bug report. They contain no text, audio or names, and they stay in memory until you quit.
- **Safe Start**, a new Hub page, is off by default. Turn it on and, if a Whisper model crashes Parla twice while loading, Parla stops loading it so the app stays usable.

## Fixed

- Settings, menus and messages throughout Parla were rewritten to be shorter and clearer.
- AI cleanup requests no longer follow HTTP redirects to another server. Before, a redirect could resend your text, and for Anthropic your API key, to a different site.
- If you started a new dictation while the previous one was still processing, and that earlier one came back empty or failed, its message could replace or hide the pill for the dictation you were recording. The pill now stays with your current dictation.

## Building from source

Still supported: `git checkout v0.7.0 && scripts/make-app.sh && open Parla.app`. A build from source can't install updates itself, since it isn't signed with the release's Developer ID, so its menu item opens the release page.

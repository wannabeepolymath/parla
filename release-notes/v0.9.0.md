The Parla window is redesigned: calmer pages, less text, and every setting in one consistent layout.

## Install

1. Download **Parla-0.9.0.dmg** below.
2. Open it and drag Parla into Applications.
3. Open Parla from Applications.

Runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13.3 or later.

## If you're upgrading

Choose **Install Update v0.9.0** in the menu bar menu. Parla downloads the new version, swaps it in and reopens. Microphone and Accessibility permissions carry over, and your settings, dictionary and history stay as they are.

## New look

- **Sidebar:** grouped into Dictation, Tools and Privacy & Help, with clearer icons. A badge shows when a permission is missing, and a red dot while a meeting is recording.
- **Every page** uses the same rows, buttons and spacing. Less-used actions sit in a **⋯** menu or the right-click menu.
- **Troubleshooting** is one page for Microphone Test, Safe Start and Diagnostics.
- **Output & Presets** is now **Rewrite Presets**. Choose what every dictation becomes (original text, cleaned up, or a preset) in **AI Cleanup → Dictation output**.
- **History** is grouped by day, with a copy button on every entry.
- **AI Cleanup** shows when your key is saved, and picks models from a list.
- **Voice Templates** has a simpler editor: placeholders follow your questions, and **Insert Field** adds them to the output.
- **Import Audio** takes a file dropped onto the page.
- **Meetings** shows elapsed and remaining time while recording, and asks before you leave the page or close the window, since that stops the recording.
- **First run** opens with a welcome, checks that Parla can hear you, then shows the shortcut you'll use every day.

## Fixed

- If settings.json can't be read, Parla now says what's wrong and offers **Reset Settings…**. The broken file is kept next to the new one as settings.json.broken-<date>, and your API keys aren't touched.

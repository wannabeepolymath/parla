Parla no longer crashes when you quit it, and installing an update always ends with the new version running.

## Install

1. Download **Parla-0.8.2.dmg** below.
2. Open it and drag Parla into Applications.
3. Open Parla from Applications.

Runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13.3 or later.

## If you're upgrading

Choose **Install Update v0.8.2** in the menu bar menu. Parla downloads the new version, swaps it in and reopens. Microphone and Accessibility permissions carry over.

## Fixed

- Quitting Parla no longer ends in a crash report. Parla now unloads its speech model before it exits. Before, macOS could show "Parla quit unexpectedly" after you chose Quit.
- After **Install Update** reaches 100%, Parla now always reopens as the new version. Before, it could stop at 100% and stay closed until you quit it from Activity Monitor.

B21's canonical experiment is accepted for explicitly reviewed, default-off ordinary physical dictation and Shortcuts result-only cleanup. Other cleanup origins retain their prior behavior. This is acceptance of bounded code/policy behavior and a controlled comparison, not certification of real providers, native UI, billing, quality or universal realtime execution.

Consent binds the exact ordered primary and backup identities to the new completion/transport contract. Existing settings decode, but prior confirmations pause for review. The eligible primary uses noninteractive credential access and bounded owned HTTP; backups require their captured, irreversibly revocable authority. No destination is discovered or added silently. Primary frozen intent survives backup-only disable; operation cancellation, deadline and participating quit/restart can abort it.

A monotonic fifteen-second cleanup budget begins before that experiment's primary credential work. It excludes capture, ASR and earlier entry preparation. Under executor progress, expiry selects raw text plus existing local rules once. Late callbacks cannot deliver another result or alter accepted history, producer metrics or another operation. Worker and watchdog remain owned and are drained before the existing serial queue marks the operation exited. Actual transport/native exit, process termination and a queued successor's recognition can remain unbounded. New ordinary starts refuse while the owner drains; a capture already underway keeps its audio and may wait or be explicitly discarded.

The six controlled cases compare raw fallback after a primary failure with ordered failover after the same primary. Both arms issue one actual compatible-client primary request with the same semantic JSON, method, URL, headers, timeout and failure schedule. Raw JSON member order may differ. Both physical and result-only origins are covered: twelve pairs, twenty-four recorded rows. Quick success adds one request and one fake-clock second; transient exhaustion adds two requests and two seconds. Terminal failure and insufficient time add no backup. Deadline and delayed-exit cases select raw at fake time fifteen; delayed real-work acknowledgement remains held until fake time forty-two. These scripted times prove decisions, not real-provider latency or benefit.

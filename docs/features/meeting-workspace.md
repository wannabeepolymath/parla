# Meeting workspace foundation

This source foundation compiles and has passed independent source review. It is outside the shipping app and supplies no native capture/recognition qualification or B43 completion credit. Corrected lifecycle/timing regressions and test oracles pass. The current virtual store/timing/directory checks passed 23 methods, and the separate 240-source/240-result capacity check passed in 296 seconds. Earlier failed and timed-out observations remain historical nonpositive results.

An explicit canonical-PCM stream entry now reuses this owner and store. It precharges two bounded slots before the source can accept input, permits one active plus one frozen/writing buffer, preserves exact PCM/WAV bytes across chunk boundaries and drains only snapshotted work after Stop or quit. Empty slots produce no audio. Overflow stops with an ordered saved prefix and unknown uncovered input, rather than adding a queue or padding silence. This is a source-level fake-port integration; no recorder, converter, Hub or termination participant is constructed by it.

The new owner is inert. Constructing it or reading its cached projection neither queries a store/clock nor schedules work. Every root, profile, bottom, wall/cooperative clock and fake capture/recognition port is explicit. There is no shipping entry, native microphone/system capture, permission request, device enumeration, converter, recognizer factory, model/provider construction, cleanup provider, History, insertion, clipboard, export or UI composition.

An explicit start validates all selected sources before starting any producer. A session selects one or two distinct tracks, initially zero; selected application and disclosed system breadth are distinct kinds. Instances, source generations, segments, epochs, sample positions and rational PTS remain separate claims. Unknown labels, mappings and coverage remain unknown. There is no inferred alignment, silence, echo cancellation, mixing or diarization. Validated declarations in the same source generation and epoch must advance without overlapping known PTS coverage across chunks and measured gaps. Source sample extents also progress within each comparable sample-rate domain; changing a segment ID does not reset an epoch. Unknown extents remain unknown and do not erase earlier known frontiers. These checks do not infer source-sample/PTS alignment. The fake-port interfaces demonstrate ownership policies; they establish no native clock/converter/foreign-thread behavior.

The actor retains its own lifetime while a checked lease or actual worker is owned, releasing it only after known committed detach or deletion. A callback is registered before queued work; Stop and Delete share one actual producer join, and at most two source callback bodies share one writer predecessor. The predecessor includes required projection IO; callback-body retirement remains a separate final action. A canceled quit never reopens ingress.

One session occupies the retained slot. The ceiling is 57,600,000 canonical 16 kHz frames per track and 240 total allocated chunks, including interrupted allocations and tails. A chunk contains 1...480,000 frames in an exact 44-byte-header PCM16 WAV, at most 960,044 bytes. Positive tails below 6,400 frames remain saved as too short for ASR. Zero/invalid data never becomes an accepted empty recording. Short/discontinuous chunks can exhaust slots before an hour.

The meeting namespace is separate, flat and explicitly injected. Its immutable profile permits depth 12, 512 entries and 524,288-byte metadata. Import remains depth 64, 64 entries, 65,536-byte metadata/replacement, its original schema/constructor/name grammar and independent 256 MiB quota. Meeting construction never infers a profile from disk. Wrong-profile, empty existing, future, unknown, aliased and unregistered states refuse and preserve files. Reading a marker to refuse is expected and is not a zero-read claim.

The closed canonical UTF-8 parser rejects duplicate/unknown keys, malformed Unicode, noncanonical spelling, null, floats, wrong booleans/integers, arity/cardinality errors and unsupported domains before granting authority. Encoded caps precede exclusive metadata writes. Ordinary, terminal and deletion tiers are 458,752 / 491,520 / 524,288 bytes. Bounds on labels, registries and detail leave a bounded terminal unknown-coverage marker and deletion progress available. Metadata projection labels previous acknowledgment; it does not freshly verify other sealed payloads.

Transient attempts/results/retirement authorities use a session-specific reserved UUID namespace with their checked allocation revision encoded in the UUID. Immutable external IDs are excluded from that reserved namespace. This enforces no reuse after an old result is retired, including after cold bootstrap, without adding a history file or changing the positional record shapes. A UUID's spelling never supplies file authority. Access still requires the closed ledger, namespace lease, exact registered identity/birth/mutation metadata and fresh selected hash. These allocation rules are included in the independent source review.

The full logical reservation is 232,581,440 bytes, below 268,435,456 by 35,854,016 bytes. It includes 240 source allocations, accepted raw UTF-8 aggregate at most 1 MiB, a single candidate **or** retired result at most 65,536 bytes, two ledgers, owner/intent and lock. Pending/sealed aliases are alternatives. There is no additional scratch or per-chunk tombstone. Uncertainty keeps slots/reservations charged. Every guarded write requires 512 MiB free headroom plus the entire still-charged envelope; guessed physical backing is never subtracted.

Each chunk has three separate durable calls: A registers/syncs the zero allocation before disk payload bytes; B writes, syncs, closes and hash-verifies that generation; C freshly verifies, renames and acknowledges the sealed generation. Results have separate reserve/write/promote/retire calls. Promotion atomically changes the pointer and retains the old accepted result as the only extra payload until verified retirement. A canceled attempt preserves accepted source/results. Non-text/failure/too-short is status, not an accepted empty raw file.

A meeting store retains one checked directory/lock lease across phases. At most one phase and one selected borrow run, with at most 14 base, two temporary and one borrowed descriptors. Each public phase has one budget: 65,536 credits, 32 validations, two enumerations, 1,024 IO iterations, 128 interrupts, a 30-second cooperative clock and reserved once-only retirement. No hard blocking-syscall wall-time promise is made. The meeting retirement path closes directories before the lock. The unchanged import lock-first close supplies no meeting retirement credit.

Every acquired descriptor is registered immediately; close consumes ownership before its result is observed and never retries that raw integer. A failed warm phase retires its temporaries, retaining the base and unrelated actual borrow. Unknown retirement poisons readiness and the registry even if the kernel may already have closed the descriptor. Only the matching borrow's actual exit may retire its existing token under quarantine, without new authorization IO. This is logical quarantine, not a physical flock-survival assertion.

Stop closes ingress, requests stop, joins actual producers and owned callback/writer bodies, commits a valid tail and publishes terminal state. It does not run ASR. Transcription is a separate explicit bounded fake consumer. Cancellation requests do not release its actual-exit or borrowed-source hold. Rejected quit releases quit intent while ingress/attempt authority stays revoked. Pending start validation blocks shutdown readiness. Borrow retirement retains its token while awaiting phase admission after actual consumer exit. Clean missing-root bootstrap remains quittable; uncertain descriptor closure refuses readiness. Shutdown requires settled storage and actual exits. No production termination/restart composition exists here.

Stop-and-delete fences first. A session/source-set/freeze/generation-bound intent must be synced and ledger-acknowledged before durable acceptance. Payload retirement then waits for actual holds and advances at most two targets per independent durable phase. The immutable target table and digest remain through all slices. Only a retired prefix/current deterministic pair may be missing; foreign replacements are not targets. Authority-dead publication precedes metadata/lock unlink, exact-empty root audit, checked root removal, parent sync and actual lease retirement. The slot is released last. Failed first intent is explicitly nondurable. After an explicitly requested metadata bootstrap, explicit deletion resumption freshly reads the matching intent and reconstructs only its generation-bound progress receipt; it neither renews expiry nor adopts a name/cache as authority. Ambiguous partial final closure preserves an unavailable charged state rather than sweeping unknown files.

Expiry is fixed at seven days from admission and never renews. Authority-bearing actions and publications evaluate the injected checked clock. Expiry revokes use while physical deletion may wait on an actual borrow or IO failure. Invalid/backwards/unrepresentable clocks refuse without destructive fallback. Metadata bootstrap does not resume producers, recognition or pending chunks; explicit interruption publication is a separate phase.
